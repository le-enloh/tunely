Rules of the Game
  1. Guess the Word:
    2. Pick an artist. You have 6 tries to guess a song name by the chosen artist.
  2. There are Hints! But there's a catch:
    3. The dictionary definition of the word will be shown once you click the "hint" button. However, you only get 3 definitions (hints) per day.
  3. Get Color Hints:
    • 🟩 Green: Right letter, right spot
    • 🟨 Yellow: Right letter, wrong spot
    • 🟥 Red: Letter is not in the word at all
  4. Use Color Hints to Improve:
    5. Use the colors from previous guesses to guide your next guess.
